Employment Application

Gila River Farms does not discriminate on any basis prohibited by federal or tribal law, and provides a preference in employment to Gila River Indian Community members, other American Indians, Alaska Natives and veterans of American Armed Forces. Gila River Farms complies with Title 12 of the Gila River Indian Community Code regarding Community member preference. It is the policy of Gila River Farms to employ qualified persons with the greatest ability, without discrimination against any employee or applicant for employment because of race, religion, color, creed, sex, disability or national origin, ancestry, age, sexual orientation, status as a disabled veteran or veteran of the Vietnam Era, or any other protected group status.
